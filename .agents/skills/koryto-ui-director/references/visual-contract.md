# Koryto visual contract

A visual change is ready only when every applicable item is true.

## Scene first

- The current location, character, event, result, or decision is the dominant reading target.
- One primary action is visually obvious.
- Supporting statistics do not precede the scene on mobile.
- No sticky or fixed panel obscures the scene, result, focus target, or action.

## Live interface

- Required text remains selectable and available to assistive technology.
- Every action is a real semantic control with an accessible name and visible focus.
- State is not communicated by color or illustration alone.
- Illustration may be cropped or simplified without removing the action or explanation.

## Responsive behavior

- Verify at 1536×864 or 1100×720 and at 390×844.
- Verify 200% zoom, long Czech labels, reduced motion, and keyboard-only navigation.
- No horizontal page overflow or unreachable control is allowed.

## Art direction

- The screen belongs to contemporary Czech local politics, not generic fantasy or generic SaaS.
- Wood, paper, brass, ink, and painterly village imagery support hierarchy rather than compete with it.
- Reused characters, factions, colors, and symbols remain consistent across screens.

## Evidence

- Capture desktop and mobile screenshots from the packaged offline build.
- Record the exact commit, viewport, route, and expected player task.
- Add a browser assertion for any changed order, focus destination, enabled action, or overflow boundary.
