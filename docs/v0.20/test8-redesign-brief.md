# TEST.8 redesign brief after human playtest 01

## Product verdict

TEST.8 is blocked. The campaign is technically functional but the player reported no fun, no felt sacrifice, no legible rival impact, and no clear difference between final strategies.

## Non-negotiable redesign rules

### 1. One irreversible sacrifice per day

Each day must present at least three plausible opportunities while allowing only two actions. At least one skipped opportunity must cause a visible and persistent consequence before the next decision:

- an ally becomes unavailable,
- a witness is intimidated,
- evidence disappears,
- a case deadline advances,
- support moves to Věčný,
- money or political debt changes,
- a route is permanently locked.

The day report must state both what the player gained and what was lost.

### 2. Věčný must mutate the board

A rival action cannot be only a report card. It must alter the next playable state by changing at least one of:

- location availability,
- action availability or risk,
- ally loyalty or safety,
- case deadline,
- evidence credibility,
- public support,
- final strategy viability.

The next screen must identify the changed element and offer at least one response action with an opportunity cost.

### 3. Strategy becomes a doctrine, not a final button

The player chooses public, legal, or worker doctrine before the final day. The doctrine must:

- unlock two exclusive preparation actions,
- lock or weaken at least one incompatible action,
- use a different primary resource,
- have a distinct failure condition,
- produce a different finale interaction and consequence.

A second playthrough must expose actions that did not exist in the first.

## Stop conditions

Do not add new locations, cases, inventory systems, campaign days, visual polish, or longer text until a human tester can answer all three questions clearly:

1. What did you sacrifice?
2. What did Věčný change?
3. How was your strategy mechanically different?

The next candidate must also fix terminal case states and embed the exact build SHA in every playtest export.
